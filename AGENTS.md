## PORTFOL I/O Landing Typography

Preserve the landing page as a centred editorial title-card composition.

- Keep the mission statement inside the `.landing__copy` centred grid lane.
- Keep `.landing__statement` centred with a restricted width, balanced wrapping, and equal side padding.
- Treat the nature stack, axis line, mission statement, and CTA buttons as separate visual layers with deliberate spacing.
- Do not let the mission statement behave like a loose paragraph or drift left/right of the central PORTFOL I/O axis.

## PORTFOL I/O Content Boxes

When editing content boxes, preserve clear separated layout lanes.

- In narrow columns, prefer a compact vertical stack: metadata, title, description, action/status, media or QR.
- Do not place images, QR codes, or media previews in a way that overlaps title or body text.
- Give embedded media its own block with visible breathing room above and below.
- Scope box-specific typography so headings inside cards do not inherit oversized section heading styles.
- Keep the archive-terminal feel: thin borders, restrained spacing, readable text, no crowded collage layout.
